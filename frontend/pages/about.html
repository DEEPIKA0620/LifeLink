About LifeLink

"Find Blood. Find Medicine. Save Lives."

LifeLink is an emergency healthcare coordination platform designed to solve critical delays in acquiring compatible blood units and essential emergency medicines during life-threatening medical situations.

Our Core Innovation

Unlike conventional directories that display static donor lists, LifeLink features an intelligent Smart Emergency Donor Matching System. Our backend algorithms evaluate compatible ABO/Rh blood groups, real-time donor availability, Haversine distance, travel time estimates, historical response rates, and donation eligibility to produce a transparent match score from 0 to 100.

Supported Stakeholders

    Patients: Submit emergency requests and track real-time donor acceptances. Voluntary Donors: Manage availability and respond to emergency requests nearby. Hospitals: Maintain live 8-blood group inventory and monitor low-stock thresholds. Pharmacies: Publish real-time medicine stock and prices for emergency locator searches.
